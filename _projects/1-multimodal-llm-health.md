Investigating interpretable and trustworthy prediction frameworks for multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs) in clinical and health monitoring applications. This work focuses on aligning structured physiological time-series signals with unstructured clinical text while providing faithful, human-understandable attributions for model predictions.
